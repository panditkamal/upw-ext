# Privacy Policy for Job Radar for Upwork

*Last updated: October 25, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **Job Radar for Upwork** ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in all our practices.

---

### 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
Job Radar for Upwork is designed with privacy-first architecture:
- We **do NOT** collect, store, or transmit your personal identifiable information (PII).
- We **do NOT** collect your Upwork login credentials, passwords, or account tokens.
- We **do NOT** track your browsing history outside of Upwork job searches.
- We **do NOT** sell, rent, or monetize your data to any third parties or advertising networks.

---

### 2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device
All operations and preferences are processed strictly on your local browser using Chrome's local storage APIs (`chrome.storage.local`):
- **User Preferences:** Your selected skills, check frequency, and alert toggles.
- **Job Feed Cache:** Temporary titles, budgets, and links of detected jobs so they can be viewed in your popup.
- **Application History:** Records of jobs you clicked or marked as applied, used solely to generate your local analytics and PDF export.

This information never leaves your personal computer or browser.

---

### 3. Permissions Usage
- `*://*.upwork.com/*`: Used to inspect publicly available job listings matching your specified skills and display client safety trust indicators directly on job pages.
- `storage`: Used to save your settings and job history locally on your device.
- `notifications`: Used to deliver desktop notifications when new matching jobs are detected.
- `alarms`: Used to trigger periodic checks at your specified interval.
- `idle`: Used to pause background checks when your computer is locked or idle to save system battery and memory.
- `activeTab` & `scripting`: Used to inspect active job listings and active filters when you click "Scan Now" or open the extension popup.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not transmit data to any third-party analytics, tracking, or marketing platforms. All AI proposal prompts are generated locally via pre-constructed templates and copied to your clipboard only upon your explicit request.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
You have complete control over your data. You can clear your job feed and proposal action history at any time directly through the Extension's interface ("Clear" button in the popup or "Reset" in settings), or by uninstalling the Extension, which permanently removes all local storage.

---

### 6. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or new features. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
